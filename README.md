# Ace Wingfield

A side-scrolling biplane dogfight that runs in the browser. Fly a fox pilot through day, night and storm skies, shoot down five kinds of sky pirate, dodge with a loop, and take on three bosses.

Play it: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ace-wingfield/

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Fly | Arrow keys or WASD | Drag on the sky |
| Fire | Space | FIRE button (auto-fire is on by default) |
| Loop (no hits mid-loop) | Shift | LOOP button |
| Pause | P or Esc | Pause button |

Turn a phone sideways for a bigger playfield.

## Power-ups

Hearts heal, stars give rapid fire, shields absorb a hit, the spread shot fires three ways, and bombs clear the screen.

## Notes

- It is a single file (`index.html`) with no build step. Your best score is saved in your browser.
- Fonts (Bungee and Fredoka, SIL Open Font License) load from Google Fonts.
- Music and sound effects are generated in the browser, so there are no audio files.
